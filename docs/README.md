# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

OctoAcme uses a structured but adaptable project management approach designed to keep cross-functional teams aligned around customer outcomes. Work moves through a clear lifecycle—initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective—so teams can move from idea to delivery with shared expectations. Each phase emphasizes practical documentation, measurable goals, and iterative delivery to keep progress visible and actionable.

Core workflows begin with defining the problem, success metrics, and stakeholder alignment in initiation artifacts, then expand into detailed planning with prioritized backlog items, milestones, dependencies, and acceptance criteria. During execution, teams rely on lightweight iteration rhythms, project boards, and pull request practices that keep work flowing through review and QA. Release and deployment are guided by checklists and rollback readiness to reduce risk while maintaining delivery speed.

Roles and personas are intentionally explicit to distribute accountability. Project Managers coordinate delivery, timelines, risks, and communications; Product Managers prioritize outcomes and validate value; Developers build and review implementation details; and QA/testing functions verify acceptance and quality standards. Stakeholders and sponsors provide direction and approvals at key decision points, helping teams stay aligned with business priorities.

Communication and quality assurance are built into the process rather than treated as separate activities. OctoAcme uses predictable cadences such as standups, weekly syncs, and stakeholder updates, plus escalation paths for blockers and incidents. Quality is reinforced through CI checks, testing layers (unit, integration, and smoke/end-to-end where needed), security scanning, release verification, and retrospective follow-through so teams can continuously improve delivery outcomes.
